# Public support information — Canada and Ontario

**Draft for independent review — not published.**

CARE-Link is a private demonstration. This page gives public support information; it does not connect you to a CARE-Link worker. An accepted help request does not confirm a staffed connection.

## Immediate danger

For immediate danger or urgent medical help, call **9-1-1**.

## 9-8-8 — Canada

If you are thinking about suicide or concerned about someone else, support is available by phone and text throughout Canada, in English and French, at any hour, every day.

- Call: [988](tel:988?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline)
- Text: [988](sms:988?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline)
- Website: [https://988.ca/](https://988.ca/?oai_link_source=model_response_hotline)

## ConnexOntario — find services in Ontario

ConnexOntario provides free information and navigation for mental-health and addiction services in Ontario. Its information service operates around the clock, every day. Conditions for individual treatment programs can differ.

- Phone: 1-866-531-2600
- Service information and other contact channels: [ConnexOntario](https://connexontario.ca/our-services/)
