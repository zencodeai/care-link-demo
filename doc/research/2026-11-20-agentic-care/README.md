# Agentic CARE-Link research resources

The user requested research before architecture/design/implementation. Primary evidence informed the selected bounded LangGraph, broker-mediated local Qwen and role-scoped knowledge design. Exact downloaded-resource URLs, dates, hashes and publisher-use limits are in [the registry](../../../config/demo/knowledge/reference-sources.json). Installed framework versions, licenses and lock digest are in [framework provenance](../../../config/demo/knowledge/frameworks.json).

The snapshot paths in the registry identify copies held in the workstation research archive. Published source uses the pinned publisher URLs and digests for reproduction.

The runtime catalog is separate from these source snapshots. It contains eight bilingual authored synthetic administrative examples plus two professional-only, reference-only Ontario Health paraphrases. The English PDF scope and shared-decision pages were text-extracted and checked; no PDF layout/design validation is claimed. French paraphrases are not publisher translations or independently language-reviewed clinical material. No reference file is automatically ingested, treated as policy or used to grant authority.

See [architecture](../../decisions/2026-11-20-agentic-workflows-and-clinical-rag.md) and [implementation/evidence](../../IMP/slices/IMP-06-bounded-ai-assistance/sessions/003-agentic-workflows-rag/).

Primary research included LangGraph workflow/persistence/interrupt documentation; LM Studio model lifecycle/tokenization/API documentation; OWASP Agentic Applications2026 and Securing Agentic ApplicationsV2; NIST AI600-1; NCSC prompt-injection guidance; HL7 FHIR Clinical Reasoning/Provenance; Ontario Health major-depression and transition standards; MIRAGE/MedRAG; CARE-RAG; and a randomized clinical documentation trial. These support design choices, not a CARE-Link clinical safety claim.
