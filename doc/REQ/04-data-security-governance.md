# Data, security and governance

> Scope update (2026-11-04): [Decision answers](08-open-decisions.md) and [demo parameters](demo-parameters.json) establish Ottawa/Ontario context and exclude all police roles, brief sinks, connectors and partnerships from the proposed demo. Earlier police-specific descriptions below are retained only as historical source context and are superseded for implementation; they do not imply a police relationship. Source FR-C6 and AQ-9/AQ-26 are excluded by current user scope, not satisfied. Concrete defaults replace earlier open engineering choices; clinical/legal approvals and hardware-dependent checks remain unresolved.

Sources: FRS §§6.8–6.12, 8; SAD §§9–12; HLD §§7–11; INV-1…INV-16. Technology choices in this document are proposed bindings; legal compliance is not assessed.

## Data requirements

**TR-DAT-01.** SHALL persist typed records with stable opaque IDs, environment/population, purpose, ownership and version metadata; use UTC timestamps and render local time explicitly. All person-related test data SHALL be synthetic, including imported fixtures and exported evidence. Authentic prototype login/account metadata SHALL be kept separate from synthetic business personas and minimized. Verify invalid population rejection at ingress and storage, fixture scanning and export review.

| Entity | Required information and lifecycle |
|---|---|
| Session / turn | Channel/role/jurisdiction, ordered events, state revision, capture/unknown/deferred assessment, stub tier history, pathway/outcome, assignment and all version stamps; resume without silent identity linkage. |
| PID / carer | Optional synthetic identifiers, relationships and voluntarily declared preferences; account identity does not imply relationship authorization. |
| Information assertion | Subject, field/value, author, source, effective date, verification and dispute status, consent/purpose; conflicting assertions retained, never silently overwritten. |
| Crisis plan | Warning signs, strategies, preferred responders, medication information as reported, advance wishes, contacts, substitute decision-maker and per-field provenance/sharing; deletion subject to approved retention exceptions. |
| Directory record | Service/catchment/hours/exclusions/age/language, police-independence and qualifications, per-field source/owner/method/time/freshness/correction route; append versions, suspend/quarantine, represent conflicts. |
| Consent ledger | Grant and revocation events, purpose/recipient/field scope/basis/session/assurance/time window; append-only history and current effective view. |
| Referral / brief | Authorized immutable payload snapshot and digest, recipient/version, consent references, stable idempotency key, generation/attempt/channel/receipt and reconciliation state. |
| Audit event | Dedicated immutable write path, sequence and hash chain, request/trace/actor/purpose/decision, input/output digests and pinned artifact versions; content excluded by default. |
| Follow-up / outcome / incident | Separate contact consent, scheduled/cancelled/sent status, pathway offered/taken/unknown outcome, source attribution; incident notification/review/action tracking. |
| Registry / policy / metrics | Artifact digests/signatures/content class and reviewer receipts; metric definitions, denominators, cohorts and unknown/missing counts. |

**TR-DAT-02.** SHALL define explicit approved demo retention values per class, purge jobs, backup retention and restore-time tombstone filtering. No invented legal deadline: FRS audit minimum is seven years for its intended deployment, whereas most other durations are unresolved. Demo configuration SHALL distinguish engineering test retention from future legally approved retention. Deletion/export SHALL require operation-specific assurance and scope, record justified exceptions and expose completion/failure status. Verify repeated jobs and restoration of a backup containing deleted/revoked records.

**TR-DAT-03.** SHALL support transactional state changes and recoverable side effects. Commit decision and audit/outbox intent durably before consequential result; external attempts/receipts are separately audited events. Audit failure SHALL prevent new consequential execution/results without removing static information or the independent human-help route. Exactly one canonical decision/action event per logical operation SHALL be enforced by unique keys, while retries and receipts may have distinct linked events. Verify crash before commit, after intent, after external acceptance and before receipt.

**TR-DAT-04.** SHALL not retain chain-of-thought or raw prompts/conversations in telemetry by default. Replay inputs that are necessary SHALL reside in explicitly scoped synthetic replay storage with retention and encryption; digest-only audit cannot reconstruct content on its own. Deterministic replay SHALL use recorded model outputs and pinned component/artifact versions; it SHALL not require regenerating identical LLM text.

## Security enforcement

**TR-SEC-01.** SHALL authenticate human and workload callers, enforce least privilege, default-deny network access, encrypt approved service connections and durable storage, and separate audit writer/reader, operator, deployer and data identities. Secrets SHALL not enter git, images, prompts, browser bundles or ordinary logs; use mounted secret references with rotation/revocation. Local mesh/IAM/key-store technology remains D-04. Test credentials SHALL still be unique and revocable.

**TR-SEC-02.** SHALL issue broker-owned short-lived capabilities bound to session, tool set, budget, expiry, jurisdiction and tier ceiling. Tokens SHALL never be provided to model/runtime context. Tool parameters SHALL resolve recipient IDs through approved sourced records; no user/model-supplied URL/number/address may become an executable destination. Schema-valid inputs do not imply authorization. Verify T-1…T-5 taint rules and capability inflation/replay/expiry negatives.

**TR-SEC-03.** SHALL bind consent to subject, authorized grantor, assurance, purpose, recipient, requested fields, session and valid time window. General role authorization and consent are separate required checks. Recheck current consent and directory suspension immediately before execution under a defined concurrency boundary; revocation before transmission authorization SHALL block the action. An in-flight completed transmission cannot be “unsent”; report accurately and stop future disclosure. Tone, silence, carer relationship and model assertions SHALL not create consent.

**TR-SEC-04.** SHALL treat user text, tool outputs, plan free text and incoming feeds as untrusted data. Envelope labels originate outside the model and cannot be promoted by model output. Retrieved facts are not instructions. Quarantine schema-invalid, poisoned, conflicting or unsigned/unverified feed records; validate safe rendering and prevent script injection. Do not fetch arbitrary uploaded URLs or execute attachments as part of baseline behavior.

**TR-SEC-05.** SHALL enforce session isolation in caches, event streams, inference context, API queries and logs; disable shared semantic memory/retrieval across sessions. Continuation SHALL be explicit, expiring, one-use or rotation-protected, and never embedded in logs/analytics. IP, caller ID and fingerprints SHALL not establish identity, linking or assurance. Verify cross-account ID guessing, token theft/replay and stream access after revocation.

**TR-SEC-06.** SHALL omit all real emergency/partner contact capabilities and credentials. Emergency guard SHALL always deny machine actors even on resolvable simulated targets; human role-play authorization SHALL only target the isolated simulator. Verify endpoint aliases, unicode targets, parameter smuggling, chained actions and malicious configuration. No configurable “enable autonomous emergency call” option.

**TR-SEC-07.** SHALL provide realtime operational views for authentication failures, forbidden tools, schema/taint rejection, consent denial, directory quarantine, unusual access, audit failure, queue failures and kill state, with traceable alerts and bounded-cardinality metrics. Event-to-dashboard/alert latency and alert thresholds require D-08. Rate limits SHALL protect expensive inference while preserving the deterministic human-help and fallback paths. Sensitive payloads SHALL not be copied into alerts.

**TR-SEC-08.** SHALL maintain a threat model and adversarial scenarios covering all NFR-17b cases, plus the FR-J prohibitions: diagnosis/prescribing, harmful method guidance, human impersonation, relationship substitution, dependency patterns, involuntary determinations, policing profiles and harmful-belief reinforcement. Output filtering/prompts alone SHALL not be credited as proof of these prohibitions; include reviewed templates, capability absence and human evaluation of residual language risks.


## Realtime security enforcement

These requirements extend TR-SEC-01…08 and share the enforcement infrastructure of TR-GOV-06…12. Security controls SHALL prevent prohibited effects inline; collection of telemetry, detection scores or later alerts alone is not sufficient. [Standards baseline and mappings](09-standards-and-runtime-controls.md) defines the reference frameworks and verification obligations.

**TR-SEC-09 — Complete runtime mediation.** Human requests, workload calls, agent steps, scheduled tasks and reconnecting streams SHALL be authenticated and authorized at each protected boundary. Enforce object/field scope, current assignment/assurance, request schema/size, allowed method/content type and output encoding server-side. Protect browser sessions against CSRF, script injection and token leakage; enforce origin rules, secure cookies and appropriate content-security headers. Reject caller-supplied authority headers. Internal network location or a valid login SHALL not imply access permission. The threat/control inventory SHALL identify every alternate path to stores, inference, connectors and administrative endpoints.

**TR-SEC-10 — Continuous identity and credential response.** On credential compromise/revocation, role or assurance change, assignment removal or workload credential expiry, SHALL deny affected subsequent operations, invalidate active subscriptions and fence queued actions. Recheck authoritative state even if an invalidation event is lost. Signed identity events SHALL be issuer/audience/subject validated, freshness bounded and replay protected; delivery SHALL support deduplication, acknowledgements and gap recovery. Use OpenID SSF/CAEP where the selected identity provider supports it, or explicitly document a local protocol with equivalent required semantics and its interoperability limits. Do not claim CAEP conformance for custom consent or policy-epoch events. Revocation SHALL not terminate access to public fallback or misrepresent a disconnected worker as connected.

**TR-SEC-11 — Network and tool containment.** SHALL enforce default-deny pod/service/host routing and destination allowlists, prevent arbitrary SSRF and DNS/redirect-based destination substitution, disable unused service-account token mounts and restrict Kubernetes administrative access. Validate allowed recipients through authoritative identifiers immediately before dispatch. Runtime-to-store, runtime-to-host-LM-Studio and runtime-to-internet bypasses SHALL be blocked outside the prompt/application layer. Disallowed police/emergency capabilities remain absent. Tests SHALL exercise IPv4/IPv6, DNS resolution, redirects and alternate host ports where enabled; unsupported address families SHALL be explicitly disabled or blocked.

**TR-SEC-12 — Inline AI input/output controls.** SHALL validate model proposals and tool outputs, preserve provenance/taint, enforce recipient/tool/field constraints, render text safely and prohibit model output from becoming executable code, SQL, shell or policy. Instruction-bearing feed/plan content SHALL not change authority. Secrets SHALL never enter prompts; service/legal/protocol claims require current approved references and controlled assembly. Optional injection classifiers may flag/restrict a turn but SHALL never grant access or replace structural enforcement. Shared memory/vector retrieval remains disabled; enabling it requires a new reviewed isolation/poisoning design. Natural-language safety residuals require human evaluation and cannot be claimed solved by schema checks.

**TR-SEC-13 — Resource and abuse control.** SHALL enforce per-identity/session/workload input, token, step, tool, concurrency and queue limits before consuming the resource. Reject oversized/decompression-abusive input and bound parser/runtime execution. Partition capacity for deterministic human-help operations from inference. Sustained abuse SHALL throttle or quarantine the affected principal/workload with an auditable reason; a single model/tool denial SHALL not automatically ban a participant or end accompaniment. Explicit human security review is required for scope expansion or release from containment where credentials may be compromised.

**TR-SEC-14 — Trusted runtime artifacts and drift response.** Before activation/startup SHALL verify approved artifact digests/signatures and identity of images, model, prompts, tools, policies and configuration. Check security-critical runtime state at readiness, every activation and periodically; detect unexpected endpoint, workload privilege, network-policy or artifact changes. Drift SHALL fence affected new operations or remove affected workload readiness, alert operators and preserve independent fallback. Scanners and SBOMs support release review but do not replace runtime mediation. Do not automatically patch models/policies or approve security exceptions with an LLM.

**TR-SEC-15 — Deterministic containment and recovery.** SHALL map validated security events to bounded responses: deny malformed/unauthorized request; quarantine poisoned record; revoke compromised principal; fence compromised workload; invoke safe mode for a critical containment breach. Record trigger, scope, actor/controller, evidence, expiry and recovery authorization. Unknown/low-confidence anomaly scores SHALL not cause unbounded environment-wide shutdown. Denial SHALL occur before effect; response propagation and dashboard/alert delays are separately measured. Recovery requires current approved artifacts, credential rotation where needed, no unresolved critical findings and an authorized human decision; it SHALL not resurrect revoked grants.

**TR-SEC-16 — Standards-traced verification.** SHALL maintain a focused versioned mapping from applicable OWASP ASVS controls, OWASP AI risk categories and MITRE ATLAS techniques to threat scenarios, enforcing component/policy, negative tests, evidence and residual risk. Include supply-chain compromise, direct/indirect prompt injection, poisoned context, exfiltration, excessive agency, unsafe output, identity abuse and resource exhaustion. No inferred ATLAS technique IDs or fabricated ASVS requirement numbers: extract exact identifiers from pinned official artifacts during implementation and validate links. Record justified non-applicability, including absent training pipelines and disabled embeddings. Security-critical bypasses block demo release, regardless of synthetic data. Coverage percentages SHALL use an explicit applicable-control denominator and not imply complete framework compliance.

## Governance requirements

**TR-GOV-01.** SHALL require GitHub PRs with human review for implementation/config/artifact changes. Protect the main branch with required checks/review and no routine direct push; exact repository settings and available review identities require D-09. Proposer SHALL not approve the same change. Automation and an agent account SHALL not count as human approval.

**TR-GOV-02.** SHALL classify changes using the source C0–C3 matrix and require the applicable clinical, equity, privacy, security and operational reviewers. Changes to model/clinical/consent/emergency/tool authority SHALL never be treated as routine UI edits. Source governance remains in its own repository; this package does not amend it. Record immutable review receipts bound to exact artifact digests, not editable approval booleans.

**TR-GOV-03.** SHALL register all release artifacts with type/version/digest, source revision, provenance, content class, population and approval status. Missing/unknown artifacts or stub content SHALL deny promotion above demo. Model/prompt/policy/directory/config versions SHALL be reconstructable per session; rollback SHALL select a compatible bundle, not independently mix arbitrary versions.

**TR-GOV-04.** SHALL couple police-avoidance metrics with safety, access and equity measures in dashboard, API and export schemas. Missing outcomes and synthetic cohort limitations SHALL be visible. No individual-granularity exposure metrics SHALL be readable by any role. Reports SHALL not claim measured clinical benefit or equity from stub scenarios. Verify serialization rejects isolated police-avoidance output and small/individual exposure groups.

**TR-GOV-05.** SHALL record incidents, reviewer actions, remediation, shadow comparisons and rollback decisions. Shadow triage changes SHALL not affect participant decisions; all evaluation is synthetic. Public reporting, independent external assessments and regulatory/clinical sign-offs remain future operational obligations, not fulfilled by an internal dashboard.


## Realtime governance policy enforcement

Governance SHALL operate as an inline runtime control, in addition to PR review, release gates and monitoring. A dashboard alert after an action is not enforcement. The following requirements extend TR-GOV-01…05 and TR-SEC-01…08; they are proposed demo requirements, not claims of implemented controls.

**TR-GOV-06 — Mandatory enforcement before effect.** Every protected human, agent, service and scheduled-job operation SHALL pass a server-side policy enforcement point (PEP) before data is read, modified, disclosed, transmitted or rendered. Direct API calls, background tasks, retries, event subscriptions and internal service calls SHALL not bypass enforcement. The broker enforces agent tools; edge/service PEPs enforce human and service actions; repository filters enforce row/field scope. Deterministic policy decision points (PDPs), proposed as OPA/Rego, return decisions; the model, UI and caller cannot grant permission. Independently retained network restrictions and absent capabilities remain effective even if application policy is defective.

| Runtime boundary | Governance policies enforced before effect |
|---|---|
| Session admission and each request | Closed demo access, synthetic population, authenticated actor, current role/assurance, session ownership, jurisdiction and release ceiling |
| Context assembly and model invocation | Allowed approved model/prompt versions, content labels, session isolation, permitted fields, token/step budgets and pinned local inference destination |
| Tool proposal and execution | Tool allowlist, capability scope/budget, current consent, declared purpose, recipient/source provenance, freshness, emergency/police exclusions and kill state |
| Record read/write, export and deletion | Assignment/ownership, row/field permissions, operation assurance, retention constraints and justified disclosure basis |
| Queue, handoff and takeover | Authorized worker assignment, exclusive conversation ownership, stale-output fencing, youth privacy and preservation of human-help access |
| Output, event stream and metrics serialization | Approved sourced claims/templates, data minimization, current recipient permissions, companion measures and aggregate-only exposure data |
| Configuration activation and administrative action | Change class, independent approvals, artifact digest/signature, environment, effective time, revocations and non-overridable demo constraints |

**TR-GOV-07 — Explicit decision contract.** Each policy request SHALL contain server-derived actor/workload identity, role/assurance, action, resource/session, purpose, jurisdiction, population/environment, requested fields, consent reference, assignment revision, relevant source/artifact digests, current governance epoch and kill state. Untrusted client/model declarations SHALL not substitute for authoritative lookups. The response SHALL include `PERMIT`, `DENY` or `REQUIRE_HUMAN`, decision ID, reason codes, policy bundle digest, state revisions, validity deadline and mandatory obligations. Missing inputs, unknown decisions and unsatisfied obligations SHALL deny the protected effect. `REQUIRE_HUMAN` routes a request for review; it is not permission. Record the resulting human authorization as a new scoped, expiring decision rather than editing the original result.

**TR-GOV-08 — Immediate execution check and race control.** A permit SHALL authorize only its exact actor, action, resource, purpose, field set and payload digest. At the defined execution-authorization boundary, the PEP SHALL verify the permit deadline and current policy epoch, revocation, consent, assignment and resource revisions. A revision change SHALL force re-evaluation. Bind transactional state writes to checked revisions; use a scoped one-use dispatch authorization for outbox work. A queued job SHALL recheck at dispatch rather than reuse an enqueue-time permit. Retries SHALL reauthorize while preserving action idempotency. Document the irreversible boundary for external effects: a revocation ordered before authorization blocks execution; an already dispatched transmission may complete and must be reported honestly. No distributed instantaneous revocation or undo of delivered content is claimed.

**TR-GOV-09 — Live activation and revocation.** Signed, independently approved policy/config bundles SHALL activate at a recorded effective time without application rebuild. Each activation SHALL advance a monotonic governance epoch, with atomic bundle switching per evaluator and acknowledged versions per enforcement instance. Restrictive revocations SHALL fence new protected operations at the central authorization boundary immediately on commit; distributed PEPs SHALL acknowledge/invalidate their previous state within the configured propagation budget. A lagging or disconnected PEP SHALL not continue authorizing with stale state. Policy relaxation SHALL wait for required approvals and healthy evaluator acknowledgements. Startup, restart and recovery SHALL fetch current effective state before readiness; rollback SHALL create a new activation epoch and SHALL NOT resurrect revoked credentials, consent, permissions or grants. Source-fixed police/emergency exclusions and the demo ceiling cannot be relaxed by a runtime bundle.

**TR-GOV-10 — Fail closed with preserved help.** Policy timeout, unavailable authoritative state, invalid bundle/signature, unacknowledged activation, stale revision or audit-commit failure SHALL block the affected protected operation and return a typed safe response. No fallback-to-PERMIT, fail-open positive cache or model-based policy decision is allowed. Public static information SHALL remain independent. An authenticated, minimal human-help request SHALL use a separately defined deterministic path with no protected record disclosure or consequential tool execution; if workforce routing is unavailable, retain the request locally where possible and explicitly report that connection is unconfirmed. This path SHALL not become a general policy bypass. Record local outage diagnostics when central audit is unavailable and reconcile them on recovery without claiming a central audit commit occurred earlier.

**TR-GOV-11 — Continuous sessions and streams.** Governance SHALL be rechecked at each protected streamed event/output, workflow transition and before effect, not just at login or session start. Revoked roles/assignments, changed sharing, directory suspension, kill state or expired grants SHALL cancel or fence affected queued work and prevent subsequent disclosure. In-progress generation SHALL not deliver stale output after takeover or policy invalidation. Already visible information cannot be recalled. Event notifications and cancellation improve responsiveness; correctness SHALL also depend on authoritative execution checks, so a dropped notification cannot preserve access.

**TR-GOV-12 — Runtime accountability and bounded latency.** Each protected logical decision SHALL commit its actor, action, permit/denial/review result, reasons, obligations, bundle digest/epoch, state revisions, correlation and actual enforcement outcome to the audit trail under TR-DAT-03. Decision, effect and receipt are distinct facts. The operator/governance UI SHALL show desired versus acknowledged policy epoch, pending activations, drift, revocations, denied actions and affected operations. Alerts supplement inline enforcement. Apply the explicit demo budgets in `runtime_governance`; measure policy evaluation, end-to-end authorization, revocation propagation and alert visibility separately under declared load. A timeout SHALL deny the action rather than exceed a budget silently. These are measured soft realtime targets, not hard realtime scheduling guarantees or established performance.

Policy enforcement cannot decide every clinical, legal or natural-language governance claim. Encode deterministic obligations explicitly; unsupported content remains stubbed or blocked, with residual human review. No language-model judgement can waive a mandatory rule.

> This prototype demonstrates control and containment architecture using non-clinical stub logic and synthetic data. It provides no evidence of clinical safety, triage validity, or equity performance, and must not be cited as such.

Agentic task records and human review receipts SHALL follow [TR-AI-04…08/15](10-agentic-ai-and-service-model.md): preserve scoped actor/task identity, source references/revisions, structured step/observation history and exact-payload approval without storing hidden model reasoning. Agentic assistance never grants authority or substitutes for runtime enforcement.
